The Republic, Plato

Major themes:  

Finding Justice
Comes from correct ordering of the soul (reason, spirted, appetite) 
Socrates finds this through analogy of the city (Guardians, Auxiliary, the producers) 
This correct ordering of the soul corresponds to a correct ordering of regimes
5 regimes: Aristocracy, Timocracy, Oligarchy, Democracy, (Anarchy), Tyranny 
The tyrannical man vs. the philosopher king
The unexamined vs. examined life
The shaping of a person to have the correct disposition towards truth happens through education
Start with Music and Gymnastic 
Then move on towards Mathematics - Geometry 
Finally ending with Dialectic which is the highest form of reasoning and allows for the apprehension of truth 
This process is modeled well with the Analogy of the Cave 
Education is the forceful removal from the cave 
Through the teaching of other which can be painful and take adjusting, one can finally see the light of truth (the sun in the analogy) 


The Ethics, Aristotle

Major themes: 

Virtue: habitual nature, mean nature, humans have the capacity for it, but not by nature
Virtue developed through education 
Having the correct disposition to pleasure and pain which comes through teaching (4)
Types of virtue: moral and intellectual 
Moral: concerned with action and feeling (think continence)
Intellectual: concerned with reason and knowledge 
Continence (knowing and doing the correct thing without enjoyment) and Incontinence (knowing the correct but doing the incorrect thing and enjoying it) 
All are aimed at a perceived good. The final good (our telos) cannot be achieved in this life 
Friendship: virtuous, pleasure, and utility 

Pride & Prejudice, Jane Austen

Pride (Darcy) and Prejudice (Elizabeth)

Happiness in marriage

Marriage for Material Gain: Collins

Summa Theologica, Thomas Aquinas

	Major themes: 
Happiness is the final end (telos)
Attained through contemplation: loving and knowing God 
Twofold nature: Happiness (thing achieved) and relationship with God (means for achieving) 
Therefore, this end cannot be achieved in this life
Happiness is self-sufficiency, unity with created and creator
All men are shot to the final end by nature (subordinate ends)
All men act towards a perceived good
