Medieval history is the period of European history that lasted from the 5th to the 15th century. It is characterized by the fall of the Western Roman Empire and the rise of the Catholic Church. The fall of the Western Roman Empire: The Western Roman Empire, which had ruled much of Europe for hundreds of years, fell in the 5th century due to a combination of internal decay and external pressures from barbarian invasions. This marked the end of the ancient world and the beginning of the Middle Ages. The rise of the Catholic Church: The Catholic Church emerged as a powerful force during the Middle Ages, playing a central role in the spiritual, cultural, and political life of Europe. The Church was led by the Pope and was organized into dioceses, each headed by a bishop. The Church played a major role in the spread of Christianity throughout Europe and was instrumental in preserving classical knowledge during the Dark Ages. The feudal system: The feudal system was a system of government that arose during the Middle Ages in which lords granted land to vassals in exchange for loyalty and military service. The feudal system was characterized by a hierarchy of lords and vassals, with the king at the top. This system allowed for the protection and organization of society, but it also led to conflicts and power struggles between different lords. The Crusades: The Crusades were a series of military expeditions launched by the Catholic Church in the 11th and 12th centuries to recapture the Holy Land from Muslim control. The Crusades were fought by knights and soldiers from Europe, who believed they were on a holy mission to defend Christianity. The Crusades had a significant impact on medieval Europe, leading to the spread of new ideas and technologies, as well as increased contact with the Islamic world. The Black Death: The Black Death, also known as the bubonic plague, was a pandemic that swept through Europe in the 14th century, killing millions of people. The disease was transmitted by fleas that lived on rats and was spread through trade and travel. The Black Death had a profound impact on medieval society, leading to social and economic upheaval and contributing to the decline of feudalism.
